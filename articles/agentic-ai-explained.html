What Is Agentic AI? A Practical Introduction

Learn how goal-directed AI systems use tools, decisions and feedback to complete multi-step tasks.

ThinkAgentic · 23 October 2026

Agentic AI refers to systems that can work toward a goal through multiple steps, often combining language models with tools, workflow logic and feedback.

How is it different from a chatbot?

A chatbot may answer a question directly. An agentic workflow can break a task into steps, retrieve relevant information, call approved tools and check whether the result meets the goal.

A simple example

Imagine a system that helps a team prepare a weekly status report. It could retrieve approved project updates, group them by topic, draft a summary and request human approval before sharing.

Architecture considerations

Useful enterprise systems need clear tool permissions, reliable data access, observability, evaluation and human oversight for consequential actions. More agents do not automatically mean better results.

Where to begin

Start with one narrow process, define success measures and assess whether simpler automation could solve the problem before introducing autonomous steps.
