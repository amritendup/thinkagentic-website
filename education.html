AI MENTORING

Guidance beyond a traditional course.

Learning AI can become confusing because the technology landscape changes quickly. There are many models, frameworks, tools and approaches, and not every learner needs to learn all of them.

Mentoring can help learners identify what to learn next, understand practical trade-offs, choose suitable projects and connect concepts with real-world applications.
