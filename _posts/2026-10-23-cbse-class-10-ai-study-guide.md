Artificial Intelligence is a school subject that rewards both conceptual understanding and practical application. A good preparation plan should cover the **official curriculum for your examination year**, not just a collection of sample questions.

## Start with the official curriculum

Download the current syllabus and assessment guidelines from your school or the board's official academic website. Make a checklist of each unit, its expected learning outcomes and any practical or project requirements. Confirm the academic year before relying on a guide.

## Build a simple study routine

1. **Understand the idea:** explain each concept in your own words.
2. **Apply it:** work through a practical scenario or classroom activity.
3. **Check your understanding:** answer short questions without looking at notes.
4. **Review mistakes:** maintain a list of concepts to revisit.

## Learn through examples

For a topic such as the AI project cycle, choose a real-world problem, identify the data that might help, discuss model choices and think about how results should be evaluated.

## Prepare for assessments

Use the latest official sample papers and school guidance when available. Practise explaining *why* an answer is correct, rather than memorising definitions alone.
