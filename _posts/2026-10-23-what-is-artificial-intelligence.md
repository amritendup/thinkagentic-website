Artificial intelligence (AI) describes computer systems designed to perform tasks that often require human intelligence, such as recognising patterns, working with language and making predictions.

## AI in everyday life

You may encounter AI in voice assistants, spam filters, translation systems and recommendations. Different applications use different techniques, and not every automated system is AI.

## AI, machine learning and generative AI

**AI** is the broad field. **Machine learning** is an approach in which systems learn patterns from data. **Generative AI** produces content such as text or images based on patterns learned during training.

## Try a small activity

List three digital tools you use. For each, ask: What task does it perform? What information might it use? What happens if it makes a mistake?

## Use AI responsibly

AI-generated responses can be incorrect or misleading. Verify important facts, protect personal information and use AI to support learning rather than replace your own thinking.
